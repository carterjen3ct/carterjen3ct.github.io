DFW Vehicle Emissions Exposure

For this project my team looked at how vehicle-related emissions are distributed across the Dallas-Fort Worth metro area and which communities are most exposed. We pulled in OSM road network data and TxDOT Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT) counts, then used kernel density analysis and hex-cell mapping in R to build an emissions exposure surface across the region. From there we ran regression against census tract-level socioeconomic variables — income, race, and population density — to see whether higher-exposure areas track with lower-income or minority communities. Group project for GEOG 469 Urban Analytics with Trent, Isaiah, Michael, and Rei.

Methods

Kernel density analysis, hexagonal grid mapping, spatial regression, census data integration.
